## Binding decisions vs experimental proposals

1. **Owner-specified, binding:** goal is independent high-capability
   owner-adapted general intelligence on SM-S948B, not a working 3B chatbot.
   No arbitrary 4B cap. No mandatory external model, subscription or Windows
   inference. Preserve independent specialist engines and original Control.
2. **Owner-specified, binding:** real neural adaptation is required, in
   addition to L0 context, L1 durable facts, L2 verified skills and L4
   architecture/foundation research. Adaptation claims require changed,
   hash-verified weights, immutable training lineage and independent held-out
   improvement or an explicit measured no-improvement result.
3. **Owner-specified, binding:** TeraBox is authorized as extended *storage*
   through existing Asterion infrastructure; it does not substitute for
   resident model memory, compute or local offline operation. No new
   duplicate credential bridge.
4. **Owner-specified, binding:** no owner-approval inference from third-party
   architecture proposals. The exact interface to proprietary closed
   models, internal training recipes and architectures remains unknown.
5. **Engineering candidate, not selection:** 7B–14B and larger mobile
   research. Keep pretrained network topology and tokenizer unchanged
   unless a separately trained compatible architecture transfer exists.
   Candidate quality must be tested as neural-only, adapted, retrieval,
   tool-augmented and integrated stages separately.

## Independent acceptance, unresolved issues

* 50,000 distinct sealed holdout evaluations, 200,000 separate stress cases,
  2,000 **real** integrated workflows and 1,000 genuinely human-reviewed
  assessments remain **not demonstrated**. Model Lab must report training,
  raw, memory, tool and full-system quality separately.
* Real adaptation weights, neural quality improvement, phone CPU/GPU/NPU
  benchmarks, million-token owner history reconstruction, TeraBox cold/warm
  retrieval, independent daemon source edit→test→commit→crash/restart and
  final Android signed release remain **open** until receipts exist.
* The Windows CPU benchmark establishes inference ability only; it does not
  prove long-context comprehension or user-intent competence.
* Preserve release hold, original signed v0.9.38 install and V3–V6/Nexus
  owner decisions until full release gates. No external training spend or
  third-party private-data transfer without authorization.
* The Claude PDF and its missing portions, complete external architecture
  source recovery, provider permission coverage, model accelerator compatibility,
  production-quality adaptation and all unanswered Nexus decisions stay open.
